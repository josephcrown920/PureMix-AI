Most Important Insight ⚠️

The “AI” itself is NOT the mastering.

The real mastering sound comes from:
	•	DSP chain quality
	•	Presets/training
	•	Reference matching
	•	Loudness strategy
	•	Genre adaptation

Even companies marketing “AI mastering” are heavily DSP-based.  ￼

Easiest “Good Sound” Strategy 🎵

Instead of training a giant neural network:

Use Reference-Based Mastering

Like:
	•	Matchering￼
	•	Ozone Match EQ
	•	LANDR style systems

Workflow:
	1.	User uploads song
	2.	User selects reference track
	3.	AI matches:
	•	tonal balance
	•	loudness
	•	stereo image
	•	dynamics

This produces MUCH better results faster.  ￼

If You Want Studio-Level Results

Core chain should usually be:
	1.	Linear phase EQ
	2.	Multiband compression
	3.	Saturation/exciter
	4.	Stereo imaging
	5.	Limiter
	6.	LUFS normalization

That’s basically what almost every mastering AI does internally.
